Phase 8 adds a mobile-friendly committee dashboard with member status counts, rehearsal access, pending absence reviews, monthly fee totals, and overdue subscription reminders.
Super Admins can update club settings, manage committee roles, transfer Super Admin access with confirmation, and roll active members into inactive or alumni status.
The admin area includes searchable, paginated audit history and a ZIP export containing member, rehearsal, attendance, payment, and audit CSV files.
Committee users can copy rehearsal and overdue fee reminders for WhatsApp; every admin action and export enforces server-side permissions and validates submitted data.
Hand test by signing in as Committee and Super Admin, checking each dashboard section, saving settings, changing roles, confirming a transfer/rollover, searching audit, and downloading the ZIP.
